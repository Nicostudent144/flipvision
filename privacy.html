Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

FlipVision ("FlipVision", "we", "us") is an iPhone app that reviews short clips of your skateboarding tricks and gives you a score, one thing to fix and a drill. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, who processes it and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your videos stay on your phone. We never upload the full video.
  • To review a try, the app sends a small set of still frames from your clip (up to 24 reduced images) plus a few details about the try. They are used only to produce your review and we do not store them after the review is done.
  • You don't create an account. The app signs you in anonymously with a random identifier. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don't show ads, we don't sell your data and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

1. Who is responsible

FlipVision is developed by Nicolas Alberdi, based in Spain, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: flipvision.support@gmail.com.

3. Service providers

We use these providers to run FlipVision. They process data on our behalf and only for the purposes above:

  • Supabase (servers in the United States): anonymous sign-in, database for review records and usage limits, and the server function that handles each review.
  • OpenAI: the AI model that looks at the frames and writes your review. Frames and try details are sent through OpenAI's API. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train their models by default and may be kept by OpenAI for a limited period (up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring before being deleted.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions and checks whether you have FlipVision Pro, using your anonymous ID and purchase data.
  • Apple: App Store payments, subscriptions and, if you allow them, notifications on your device.

Some of these providers are located in or transfer data to the United States. Where the GDPR applies, these transfers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Performance of our contract with you: reviewing your tries, anonymous sign-in, subscriptions.
  • Legitimate interests: preventing abuse, keeping the service secure, improving review quality and controlling costs.
  • Legal obligations: for example, tax and accounting records related to purchases (handled mainly by Apple).

5. How long we keep data

  • Frames: not stored after the review is returned.
  • Review records, feedback and usage data: while you use the app, and deleted or anonymized within 12 months of your last activity, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Purchase data: as long as needed to manage your subscription and meet legal obligations.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it in the app ("Delete all my data") or uninstall the app.

6. Permissions

  • Camera: to record your tries.
  • Photos: to pick a clip from your library and to save share cards, only if you choose to.
  • Notifications: optional local reminders (for example, to keep your streak). They are scheduled on your device.

You can change these permissions at any time in your iPhone Settings.

7. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We don't use third-party advertising or tracking, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't use your frames to identify you or to recognize faces.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the CCPA in California), you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where we rely on it. Because we don't know who you are, include the approximate dates and tricks of your reviews so we can find your records. Write to flipvision.support@gmail.com. We won't discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If you are in the EEA, you can also complain to your data protection authority; in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

9. Children

FlipVision is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are under the age required in your country to consent to data processing, use the app with a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we'll delete the related data.

10. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our database is restricted, and each install can only read its own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We'll change the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
