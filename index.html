FlipVision Support

Film a try. Get a score. Know exactly what to fix.

Need help? Email flipvision.support@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within 2 business days.

How do I get a good review?

  • Film from the side or at 45°, with your whole body and the board in frame.
  • For ledges and rails, film along the obstacle so the approach and exit are visible. For ramps, film from the side with the coping in frame.
  • Trim the clip to just the try (10 seconds max). Slow motion helps.

The app said it couldn't see a trick

That happens when the skater or board isn't clearly visible, or the obstacle is out of frame. Those tries don't use your free review. Film again following the tips above.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions > FlipVision and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your free trial or current period ends to avoid being charged. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I changed phones and lost my subscription

Open the app on your new phone, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Where are my videos?

Your videos stay on your phone. Only a few still frames are sent to review each try, and they aren't stored. Read our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings > Delete all my data. To also delete the review records on our servers, email us with the approximate dates and tricks of your reviews.
