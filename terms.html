1. What FlipVision does

FlipVision lets you record or upload a short clip of a skateboarding trick. The app sends still frames from the clip to an AI model, which returns a score, feedback on each phase of the trick, one suggested correction and a drill. The app also includes a trick library, progress tracking (XP, ranks, streaks) and a history of your tries.
